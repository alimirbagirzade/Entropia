==============================================================================
WHY THE POSITIVE activate->deprecate LEG STAYS A SKIP — the three-way lock
==============================================================================
1. VALIDATABLE_RESOLVER_KEYS = ['ta.ema', 'ta.rma', 'ta.rsi', 'ta.sma', 'ta.vwap', 'ta.wma']
   Anything outside this set fails closed by design (doc 09 §7).
2. apps/seed.py::_ESP_TA_RESOLVERS seeds ta.sma/ema/rma/atr/rsi/wma/vwap
   as TRUSTED_ACTIVE under SEED_ESP_TA=1 — which the flows stack sets,
   because the browser layer's Pre-Check needs them resolvable.
3. esp/state_machine.py::_ALLOWED — activation legality:
     candidate        -> ['trusted_active', 'unavailable']
     trusted_active   -> ['deprecated', 'unavailable']
     deprecated       -> ['unavailable']
     unavailable      -> []

   Activation is legal ONLY from `candidate`; `deprecated` is terminal
   apart from `unavailable`. A key that is already trusted_active can
   never be re-activated, and a deprecated one can never come back.

=> INTERSECTION IS EMPTY. On a SEED_ESP_TA stack there is no canonical
   key that is both validatable and activatable, and no ordering of
   harness calls creates one. The SKIP is STRUCTURAL, not laziness.

   Closing it would need a second Compose stack seeded WITHOUT
   SEED_ESP_TA (a new 12-container CI job for one assertion) or a change
   to seed.py — product code, out of scope for a CI-wiring slice.
   In-process coverage remains: tests/integration/test_esp_persistence.py
